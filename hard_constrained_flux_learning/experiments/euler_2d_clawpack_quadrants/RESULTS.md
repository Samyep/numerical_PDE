# 2-D Euler quadrant results

## Bottom line

The first prospective 2-D Euler test is physically successful but not yet
accuracy-competitive with native PyClaw Roe-64.

- HCFL improves its own dimension-by-dimension HLLC-64 base by **25.3%** NMAE
  on the held-out ID set and **7.90%** on the official quadrant OOD case.
- HCFL does **not** beat PyClaw Roe-64: its NMAE is 69.8% higher on ID and
  41.8% higher on the official OOD case.
- Every HCFL trajectory completes with positive density and pressure.  The
  hard interface-entropy residual, fully-discrete entropy balance, and
  conservation audit all pass.
- The HLL deployment safety endpoint is inactive in all 4,508 evaluated
  sample-substeps.  Thus the reported HCFL predictions are not secretly the
  low-order fallback.
- HCFL develops visible grid-aligned oscillations on the long OOD rollout.
  The official density total-variation excess is 14.9% on average over seeds.

Consequently this experiment supports the hard-constraint mechanism and a
learned improvement over HLLC, but it does **not** support a paper claim that
the current 2-D architecture outperforms a strong Roe wave-propagation method.

## Frozen design

The public test is the Clawpack 5.10
[2-D Euler quadrant gallery case](https://www.clawpack.org/gallery/pyclaw/gallery/quadrants.html).
The protocol, source states, boundary condition, model-selection rule, and
metrics were committed before any 2-D Euler model was trained or evaluated.

Random training, validation, and ID-test trajectories use independent
four-quadrant states generated by classic PyClaw Roe with two transverse-wave
corrections on `256 x 256`, then conservatively restricted to `64 x 64`.

| split | trajectories | saved states | horizon | RNG seed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| training | 48 | 21 | 0.10 | 81001 |
| validation | 12 | 21 | 0.10 | 82001 |
| held-out ID | 12 | 21 | 0.10 | 83001 |
| official OOD | 1 | 11 | 0.80 | fixed public case |

The 48 training trajectories contain 960 adjacent-time training pairs and
about 3.93 million cell-time examples, but these local examples are strongly
correlated within only 48 global trajectories.  The official test is severe
OOD in both state range and rollout length: its minimum initial pressure is
`0.0290` versus a training sampling lower bound of `0.4`, its speed reaches
`1.206` versus `0.6`, and its horizon is eight times longer.

The formal reference is the same PyClaw configuration on `1024 x 1024`,
conservatively restricted to `64 x 64`.  Reference convergence is not treated
as exactness:

| comparison against reference-1024 | rollout NMAE | rollout NRMSE | final NMAE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| reference-256 | 0.02347 | 0.12425 | 0.04244 |
| reference-512 | 0.01167 | 0.06008 | 0.02600 |

All coarse methods start from exactly the same restricted reference initial
state; the maximum initial difference is exactly zero.

## Model selection and convergence

The model is HLLC plus a signed four-wave Roe correction, followed by the hard
Tadmor half-space projection.  A feasibility penalty of `1e-3` acts on the raw
proposal.  The low-order HLL flux is absent from training and appears only as
the deployment safety endpoint.

Four- and six-cell normal stencils were compared with seed zero using only
validation NMAE:

| stencil | parameters | best update | validation completion | validation NMAE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 cells | 6,772 | 500 | 100% | 0.029662 |
| 6 cells | 7,348 | 500 | 100% | **0.029313** |

The six-cell model was selected before the official test was unlocked.  Its
three seeds all reached their best validation checkpoint at update 500 and
continued to update 6,500, where the fixed learning-rate/plateau rule stopped
them at the minimum learning rate.  Their best validation NMAEs were
`0.029313`, `0.030622`, and `0.030178`.  The subsequent validation degradation
despite lower minibatch loss is direct evidence of overfitting; the selected
checkpoints are early-stopped validation optima, not the final training states.

## Accuracy

NMAE is the mean absolute primitive-variable error after normalization by the
training-channel standard deviation.  NRMSE uses the same `(rho,u,v,p)`
channels and scale.  HCFL is mean over three seeds; `±` is between-seed
standard deviation, not variation over spatial cells.

| split | method | completion | NMAE | NRMSE |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| held-out ID | PyClaw Roe-64 | 100% | **0.01663** | **0.05262** |
| held-out ID | HLLC-64 | 100% | 0.03778 | 0.10027 |
| held-out ID | HCFL-64 | 100% | 0.02824 ± 0.00038 | 0.08054 |
| official OOD | PyClaw Roe-64 | 100% | **0.07628** | **0.30668** |
| official OOD | HLLC-64 | 100% | 0.11742 | 0.39363 |
| official OOD | HCFL-64 | 100% | 0.10814 ± 0.00689 | 0.36021 |

Raw official-OOD rollout MAE confirms that the aggregate result is not caused
by one hidden channel:

| method | density | x velocity | y velocity | pressure |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| PyClaw Roe-64 | **0.02241** | **0.03610** | **0.03527** | **0.02380** |
| HLLC-64 | 0.03912 | 0.05202 | 0.05227 | 0.03947 |
| HCFL-64 | 0.03664 | 0.04820 | 0.04841 | 0.03506 |

Among individual HCFL seeds, seed zero has the best official NMAE (`0.09866`)
and seed two the worst (`0.11482`); all three remain worse than Roe-64.  The
figures display seed zero only and label it explicitly.  The table and claims
always use all three seeds.

## Physical audit

Across all ID and official HCFL evaluations:

| check | result |
|---|---:|
| completed trajectories | 39 / 39 |
| minimum density | 0.12295 |
| minimum pressure | 0.01307 |
| maximum hard interface residual | 7.44e-8 |
| maximum relative conservation closure | 6.58e-9 |
| positivity fallback activations | 0 / 4,508 sample-substeps |
| entropy fallback activations | 0 / 4,508 sample-substeps |
| largest recorded entropy balance | negative (admissible) |

This is an important positive result: the hard projection is active as the
forward definition, yet the saved best models need neither the positivity nor
the fully-discrete low-order blend on the evaluated trajectories.  The
accuracy result therefore cannot be attributed to silently returning HLL.

## Oscillation diagnosis

The final density maps and line cuts show horizontal and vertical bands in the
HCFL solution.  Mean density TV excess is 9.09% on ID and 14.93% on the
official OOD case; PyClaw Roe-64 and the more diffusive HLLC-64 have no positive
TV excess relative to the restricted reference under this metric.

The most plausible mechanism is an architectural mismatch:

1. the HCFL network sees only a normal 6-cell stencil and predicts x/y face
   fluxes independently;
2. the PyClaw target and Roe-64 comparator use two explicit transverse-wave
   corrections;
3. therefore the local learned face flux lacks transverse information needed
   to reproduce the genuinely two-dimensional interaction;
4. the short `t=0.1` one-step training objective does not penalize the
   grid-aligned error accumulated by `t=0.8`.

This is an inference from the architecture and visual/TV evidence, not a
proved causal attribution.  It should be tested with a predeclared transverse
ablation rather than tuned against this now-observed official test.

## Claim boundary and next experiment

Supported now:

- exact finite-volume conservation to numerical tolerance;
- positive and entropy-admissible HCFL rollouts on all evaluated cases;
- no reliance on the deployment low-order fallback;
- a learned accuracy improvement over the matching dimension-by-dimension
  HLLC base.

Not supported now:

- superiority over native PyClaw Roe-64;
- suppression of 2-D grid-aligned oscillations;
- a general 2-D accuracy advantage over mature finite-volume solvers.

The scientifically valid next step is a **new prospective experiment** with a
transverse-aware but still conservative face model, for example a shared
`3 x 6` oriented patch or a learned transverse fluctuation coupled to the Roe
basis.  The hard face projection and proposal-feasibility loss can remain
unchanged.  Hyperparameters must be selected on newly generated validation
cases, and the final claim must use a new held-out quadrant configuration;
rerunning and tuning on this official case would make it exploratory only.
