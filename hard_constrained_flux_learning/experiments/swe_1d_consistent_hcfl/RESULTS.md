# SWE 1D consistent-HCFL results (seed 0)

## Frozen protocol

- Equation: homogeneous, flat-bed 1D shallow-water equations with
  `U = (h, hu)` and primitive network inputs `(h, u)`.
- Methods: HLL + learned Roe-wave correction; central flux + bounded
  nonnegative Roe dissipation + proposal-feasibility loss.
- Stencils: interface-centred 4-cell and 6-cell only. No 5-cell model was
  trained or evaluated.
- Training data: 580 periodic trajectories (220 ordinary, 160 broad, 200
  Froude-coverage), generated by HLL + SSP-RK2 on 1024 cells and
  conservatively restricted to 64 finite-volume cell averages.
- Validation data: 136 independently seeded trajectories with the same three
  categories (44, 32, 60). Checkpoint selection used validation rollout NRMSE
  only.
- Deployment tests: four fixed periodic and four fixed transmissive cases on
  512 cells through `t = 0.025`. Metrics use an HLL + SSP-RK2 2048-cell
  reference conservatively restricted to 512 cells. The figures retain the
  raw 2048-point reference curve.
- Nonperiodic boundaries: constant-extrapolation states and physical SWE
  boundary fluxes. The neural network is used at interior interfaces only.

Training coverage contains both propagation directions: 81.20% subcritical,
9.05% right-going supercritical, and 9.74% left-going supercritical states;
the maximum absolute Froude number is 2.016.

## Validation-controlled convergence

| Method | Cells | Parameters | Best update | Stop update | Best validation NRMSE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| HLL + Roe correction | 4 | 6,050 | 6,000 | 11,000 | 0.04427 |
| HLL + Roe correction | 6 | 6,338 | 7,200 | 12,200 | 0.04139 |
| Central + nonnegative Roe + feasibility | 4 | 6,050 | 7,000 | 9,000 | 0.04355 |
| Central + nonnegative Roe + feasibility | 6 | 6,338 | 7,000 | 11,000 | **0.03897** |

All four stopped on the preregistered validation plateau at the minimum
learning rate; none reached the 50,000-update cap. The 6-cell versions are
better on the independent 64-cell validation distribution.

## Held-out 512-cell deployment accuracy

| Method | Periodic mean NRMSE | vs HLL-512 | Transmissive mean NRMSE | vs HLL-512 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Native HLL-512 | 0.02316 | — | 0.01014 | — |
| HLL + Roe, 4-cell | 0.01506 | 35.0% lower | 0.00610 | 39.8% lower |
| HLL + Roe, 6-cell | 0.01918 | 17.2% lower | 0.00587 | 42.1% lower |
| Central + nonnegative Roe + feasibility, 4-cell | **0.01164** | **49.8% lower** | 0.00578 | 43.0% lower |
| Central + nonnegative Roe + feasibility, 6-cell | 0.01403 | 39.4% lower | **0.00527** | **48.0% lower** |

Thus 6 cells are not uniformly superior: they win validation and the
nonperiodic average, while 4 cells win the long periodic average in both model
families. This is a single-seed screening result, not a multi-seed ranking.

## Safety and fallback audit

- Minimum depth remained positive in every rollout. The conservative depth
  limiter never intervened (`mean alpha = 1` in every held-out case).
- Maximum post-projection interface entropy residual was below
  `2.24e-13`.
- Periodic total entropy change and nonperiodic boundary-aware entropy-balance
  residual were nonpositive at every accepted substep.
- Mean fully-discrete entropy blend `beta` was 0.84–0.91 on periodic averages
  and 0.79–0.91 on transmissive averages. The learned flux therefore did not
  collapse to the HLL fallback, but several smooth/exit cases used substantial
  blending; the per-case values are recorded in `deployment_metrics_seed0.csv`.
- Learned coefficients are nonzero. For example, the 4-cell HLL model has a
  mean absolute Roe correction coefficient of about 0.85 on the held-out
  smooth-wave trajectory.
- At a raw proposal-residual tolerance of `1e-8`, every periodic proposal set
  has zero violations. On the transmissive suite the violation rates are
  0.106%/0.084% for HLL 4/6, versus 0.015%/0% for feasibility-trained central
  4/6. The hard projection remains active in the forward path regardless.

## Important limitation: shock ringing

The accuracy improvement is real, but it is not a nonoscillatory result.
Relative to the HLL-2048 reference, learned methods add total variation and
local extrema near strong discontinuities:

| Method | Periodic mean positive TV excess | Periodic excess extrema | Transmissive mean positive TV excess | Transmissive excess extrema |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Native HLL-512 | 0.1% | 0 | 0.1% | 0 |
| HLL + Roe, 4-cell | 85.4% | 238 | 36.0% | 56 |
| HLL + Roe, 6-cell | 86.0% | 236 | 25.2% | 46 |
| Central + nonnegative Roe + feasibility, 4-cell | **40.0%** | **129** | 39.8% | 48 |
| Central + nonnegative Roe + feasibility, 6-cell | 46.7% | 150 | **19.7%** | **38** |

The worst ringing occurs in the periodic supercritical and counterflow cases;
the exit cases are visually smooth. Hard entropy stability and positive depth
do not imply TVD/monotonicity. Consequently these checkpoints support the
claim “lower average error under hard safety constraints,” but not the claim
“shock-oscillation free.” A shock-local monotonicity sensor/limiter is the next
required ablation before choosing a paper default.
