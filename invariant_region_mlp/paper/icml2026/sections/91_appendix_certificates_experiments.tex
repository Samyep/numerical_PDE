\section{Certificates Used in the Broader Benchmarks}
\label{app:certificates}
\paragraph{Rosenbrock HJB.}
For $u_t+\Delta u-\norm{\nabla u}^2=0$ and $g(x)=\log(1+x^\top Ax/2)$,
\[
\nabla g(x)=\frac{2Ax}{1+x^\top Ax},\qquad
\norm{\nabla g(x)}\le\sqrt{\lambda_{\max}(A)}.
\]
The Hopf--Cole representation carries the same envelope backward in time. With the benchmark convention $z=\sqrt2\nabla u$ and coefficient range giving $\lambda_{\max}(A)\le7.5$, we use $\norm z\le\sqrt{15}$.

\paragraph{Neufeld--Wu.}
The public example has linear drift $\mu(x)=0.06x$, additive diffusion, a 1-Lipschitz terminal payoff, and a thresholded driver proportional to $(\norm z_\infty-25)_+$. Flow sensitivity gives $\norm{\nabla u(t,\cdot)}\le e^{0.06(T-t)}\le e^{0.015}$ over the benchmark horizon, well below the activation threshold.

\paragraph{Nonlinear funding.}
For the 100D borrowing/lending benchmark, a model-derived delta envelope yields
\[
\sum_{i=1}^d\left(\frac{z_i}{\sigma x_i}\right)^2
\le \exp(\sigma^2(T-t)),
\]
which defines a state-dependent ellipsoid in normalized delta coordinates.
