% Main-text insert: structural certificate
\paragraph{Why is this subspace certified? A control interpretation.}
The Hamiltonian is the support function of the unit ball in the last
$d-1$ coordinates:
\begin{equation}
 f_d(p)=\sup_{\substack{a_1=0\\\|a\|_2\le1}} a^\top p.
\end{equation}
Hence the counterexample PDE is the HJB equation of
\[
 dX_s=a_s\,ds+dW_s,
 \qquad a_{s,1}=0,
 \qquad \|a_s\|_2\le1,
\]
with terminal reward $g_d(X_1)=|X_{1,1}|$. The controller cannot move the
first coordinate, while the reward reads only that coordinate. Thus every
admissible control produces the same terminal payoff distribution and
\[
 u_d(t,x)=\mathbb E|x_1+W_1(1)-W_1(t)|.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \partial_{x_2}u_d=\cdots=\partial_{x_d}u_d=0,
 \qquad
 \nabla u_d(t,x)\in S_d:=\operatorname{span}(e_1).
\]

\paragraph{The rescue is a property of the retraction, not of one projector.}
Since
\[
 f_d(z)=\|z_{2:d}\|_2=0\qquad\text{for every }z\in S_d,
\]
any recursive correction---including a batch-coupled one---that returns every
child gradient to $S_d$ before the parent evaluates $f_d$ makes the nonlinear
full-history corrections vanish pathwise. Samplewise IR uses $Q_dz_i$.
A batchwise structural version may use
\[
 \widetilde z_i=\alpha_BQ_dz_i,\qquad0\le\alpha_B\le1.
\]
Both therefore exact-rescue the nonlinear feedback. Pure common scaling
$\alpha_Bz_i$ by itself is not sufficient because it leaves inactive
coordinates nonzero.

% Appendix insert: short verification/uniqueness intuition
\paragraph{Verification intuition.}
Write the HJB equation as
\[
 w_t+\tfrac12\Delta w+
 \sup_{\substack{a_1=0\\\|a\|_2\le1}} a^\top\nabla w=0.
\]
For a sufficiently smooth solution $w$ and any admissible controlled
diffusion $X^a$, It\^o's formula gives
\[
 dw(s,X_s^a)=
 \bigl(w_t+\tfrac12\Delta w+a_s^\top\nabla w\bigr)ds
 +\nabla w^\top dW_s.
\]
Since $a_s^\top\nabla w$ is bounded above by the Hamiltonian supremum, the
drift is nonpositive, hence $w(t,x)\ge\mathbb E g_d(X_1^a)$ for every
admissible control. A maximizing feedback gives equality. For the nonsmooth
terminal datum $|x_1|$, the rigorous counterpart is the standard
viscosity-solution dynamic-programming/comparison argument for this globally
Lipschitz Hamiltonian.
